This is the app's own layout, running on sample data. Hover a row, open an app, switch tabs.

The dashboard, without the dashboard.

App Store Connect is a website built for one app at a time. Hermes is a Mac app built for all of them at once.

Every app at a glance

Icons, names and the live version on each platform, with a dot for where it stands: on sale, in review or rejected.

Release status

One line that says what to do next and what is in the way. Submit, cancel and see every past review, rejections included.

Builds and TestFlight

Every build with its processing state, and your TestFlight groups beside them.

Reviews and replies

Ratings and reviews in one list. Reply right there, with a signature you set once.

Store text and screenshots

Every language side by side with Apple's character limits. Screenshots by device, upload and delete in place.

Native and fast

SwiftUI on your Mac. Your key stays on your machine. No account with us, no server in between.

Built on asc

Agents can ship too.

Hermes drives the open source asc command line tool. Everything you see in the window is a command your scripts, CI and coding agents can run themselves.
